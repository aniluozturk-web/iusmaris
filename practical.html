— Getting Here —

Travel

Copenhagen Airport → Lund

Øresund train from Copenhagen Airport

The Øresund train, operated by Skånetrafiken, departs from the underground station beneath Copenhagen Airport and runs directly to Lund Central. Departures occur approximately every twenty minutes; the journey takes between thirty-five and forty minutes, crossing the Øresund Bridge.

From Lund Central, the Faculty of Law lies a ten-minute walk eastward through the old town.

Lund → Malmö

Between the two days

On the morning of 9 June, a southbound Øresund or Pågatåg service from Lund Central connects to Malmö Central. Departures occur every few minutes; the journey takes approximately twelve to fifteen minutes.

From Malmö Central, the World Maritime University lies a ten-minute walk southward along the waterfront.

About

A reading of the 2024 and 2025 climate advisory opinions of ITLOS, the ICJ, and the Inter-American Court of Human Rights, against the practice of maritime law. Held over two days in Lund and Malmö.

Funded by

The Rune and Lena Lavin Foundation for Legal Research

Auspices

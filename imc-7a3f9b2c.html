Dietary requirements, photography, and the dinner on 8 June.

We would be most grateful if you could complete the short form below at your earliest convenience. The form gathers three short matters we need in advance of the workshop: your dietary requirements for the catering across both days, your preference on photography during the proceedings, and the dinner on the evening of 8 June.

It would be an honour to host you for dinner on the evening of 8 June, following the close of Day 1. The convenors will arrange the table and confirm the location in good time, and all arrangements connected to the dinner are taken care of on the workshop's side.
